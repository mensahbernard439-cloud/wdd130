Dry Oar Rafting

We run guided white water rafting trips focused on safety, teamwork, and unforgettable river scenery for first-time paddlers and experienced adventurers alike.

History

Founded by river guides passionate about sharing wild canyons responsibly, Dry Oar Rafting grew from a single wooden oar and two rafts into a trusted outfitter known for skilled crew training and river stewardship.
